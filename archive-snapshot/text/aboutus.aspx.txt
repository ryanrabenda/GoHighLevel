About Our Company

Tarzan Treescaping has proudly been serving the Hudson Valley for over 10 years. We work closely with our clients to actualize their ideas and ensure that they have a role in the creative process every step of the way. We work with trained Horticulturalists and Arborists to provide the best advice on the types of trees to plant and we work efficiently with state-of-the-art machinery for all our work.

In order to keep our prices down, we have learned from experience that it is important to have partnerships with local providers. Thus, over the years we have developed partnerships with local businesses for most of the materials we use, making our materials easy to obtain and keeping our costs low.

We are licensed by the Landscape Contractors Association.
